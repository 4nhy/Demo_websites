**Sourcing note:** two earlier candidates were downloaded and rejected after visual
inspection — a photo that turned out to be Audio-Technica ATH-M50x (visible logo on both
earcups) and one that turned out to be literal Apple EarPods (instantly recognizable
silhouette) — both would have broken the brief's "no real brand" rule. Final set is three
verified-clean photos (no visible logos, no real recognizable product design, no
identifiable faces), covering A1/A10, A6/A7/A9/A2, and A4 via crops and CSS filters.

**Totals:** 0 rows are *shoot*. 3 unique stock photos cover 6 rows via crop/CSS-filter
reuse; 2 rows are pure CSS-SVG. Four colorways from two base photos via CSS tinting is a
real compromise, not four true product renders — flagged plainly rather than presented as
if four colorways were photographed. Fallback for a muddy tint result is a flat swatch
chip, no product photo at all, which stays honest rather than shipping a bad fake.
