( MATERIAL MASTERCLASS & JOINERY METHODOLOGY )

Woodwork Masterclass

An exhaustive examination into our workshop techniques, timber seasoning protocols, structural physics, and traditional joinery methods that guarantee multi-decade integrity.

( TOPIC 01 / JOINERY METHODOLOGY )

Mortise & Tenon Precision

We completely reject mechanical screws and synthetic adhesives in our primary structural frames. Every joint relies on traditional hand-chiseled mortise and tenon assemblies, locked with turned oak dowels that expand and contract harmoniously with ambient humidity.

( TOPIC 02 / TIMBER CURING )

24-Month Air Seasoning

Prior to entering our workshop, raw timber logs are stacked and air-dried in open Scandinavian shelters for two full years. This patient curing process eliminates internal moisture tension, preventing surface checks or warping once shaped into architectural objects.

( TOPIC 03 / SURFACE PATINA )

Cold-Pressed Linseed & Beeswax

Our signature finish involves three manual applications of cold-pressed Scandinavian linseed oil followed by a protective layer of organic Danish beeswax. This preserves the tactile warmth of raw wood while offering natural spill protection.

( TOPIC 04 / MILLING SELECTION )

Radial Quarter-Saws & Grain Alignment

To achieve supreme dimensional stability and display vertical ray fleck figure, all timber is strictly quarter-sawn perpendicular to the log growth rings. This labor-intensive milling technique yields straight grain patterns that resist cupping for centuries.

( TOPIC 05 / ADVANCED JOINERY )

Blind Dovetail Geometry & Friction Joints

For credenzas and monolithic cabinets, concealed blind dovetails are carved by master joiners. Wood fibers interlock under physical tension, creating joints that become structurally stronger as atmospheric pressure fluctuates.

( TOPIC 06 / STRUCTURAL PHYSICS )

Mass Calibration & Load Balancing

Every dining surface and armchair undergoes center-of-gravity mass calibration. Solid timber legs are offset by precise angles to distribute multi-hundred-kilogram loads across the floor grid without mechanical stress points.
