( STUDIO MONOGRAPH & PHILOSOPHY )

Architectural Manifesto

An insight into our design ethos, Nordic heritage, and commitment to permanent, uncompromised monolithic furniture objects.

( CHAPTER 01 / ORIGINS )

Founded in Restraint

House of Oak was established in Helsinki to bridge brutalist structural geometry with Nordic timber traditions. We consider furniture not as transient decor, but as permanent architectural elements that define domestic volume and spatial psychology.

( CHAPTER 02 / DESIGN ETHOS )

Brutalist Geometries

Our visual language fuses Swiss typographic principles with monolithic furniture silhouettes. Uncluttered lines, heavy proportions, and unvarnished natural surfaces invite light and shadow to articulate interior architecture.

( CHAPTER 03 / SUSTAINABILITY )

Forest Stewardship

Every log utilized in our workshop is fully traceable to certified managed forests in Finland and Sweden. For every mature tree harvested for our collection, three saplings are planted, ensuring a fully regenerative forestry cycle.
